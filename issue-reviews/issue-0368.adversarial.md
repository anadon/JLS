The Completion Criteria explicitly require "**Every cited evidence document and permalink resolves on the default branch at close**" — right now it fails that test outright, not just as a future risk. Every number this issue prints (the D-A7 determination, crossing policy P-b, the whole 4-6 mw cost band) traces to a document a `master`-only reader cannot open. Recommendation: merge or cherry-pick `docs/plan/evidence/` onto `master` before this issue does any more filing/estimating work built on top of it, or stop treating the citations as load-bearing.

**2. (High) The parent feature's step-cap design describes a capability its only planned task does not build, and no acceptance criterion catches the gap.** §3 lays out three step-cap regimes and states "the unconditional conservative cap **must not ship alone**" — phrasing that implies it does ship, just not first, and §6 calls the ordering "sequencing, not optimization" (i.e., regime 3 is real future work, not dropped). But the sole child that would implement this, #434 (TASK-0102), builds only regimes 1 and 2 and states outright that the third case ("asynchronous `Dac` drive") is "**not built**," named only in a javadoc, with no re-entry trigger given anywhere. `planned_tasks` in #368 lists exactly TASK-0102 plus the (unfiled, task-id-less) domain alphabet — neither delivers regime 3. None of §5's seven Integration Criteria exercises the async-driven-`Dac` case. A student who drives a `Dac` from an unclocked digital source gets whatever #434 decides ("refuse by name," per its own text) instead of the safe-but-slow fallback §3 of the parent describes as part of the shipped design. This is either a stale parent description or an unaccounted scope loss — either way it isn't reconciled anywhere in the tracker.

**3. (Medium) The cross-issue accounting obligation Open Question 2 imposes is unmet, twice over.** #368 requires the "who pays for the port/net descriptor — domain, radix, or strength" decision be "recorded on FEAT-027 (#341) and FEAT-028 (#344) as well as here," and the Aug 4 boundary-note comment on #368 repeats the same demand. The DoD checklist item "Machine block, roster table, and mermaid graph agree with reality at close" is being violated *before* close, not merely at risk of it, and nothing in the issue prompts fixing the body now that #434 exists.

## Recommendation

Not a redesign — the ownership-inversion premise and the domain-check-ordering argument are sound and well-grounded in the actual codebase. But this issue should not be treated as ready to fund until: the evidence documents are actually merged to `master`, the step-cap regime-3 gap between this issue and #434 is reconciled (either #368's text is trimmed to match what #434 ships, or a new acceptance criterion covers the async-`Dac` case), and the Open Question 2 cross-posting to #341/#344 actually happens.
