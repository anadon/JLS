# CAP-12 (#305) readiness review

**Verdict: not-ready** — the capstone body itself is well-formed, current, and internally consistent, but its redirected block-chain design is not reflected anywhere in the filed feature/task corpus. Two of the five required features (#331, #318) were independently rewritten to scopes that no longer deliver what CAP-12 requires them for, a third (#368) is filed against the solver design CAP-12 explicitly excludes, and the capstone's central deliverable — the conditioning-block palette — has no filed owner at all. The dependency edges as filed put FEAT-046 (#351), which CAP-12's machine block explicitly removed, back on the critical path of two required features. Decomposition and reconciliation work is needed before implementation can be orchestrated from these issues.

Reviewed 2026-08-16 against: issue bodies fetched via the GitHub REST API; native sub-issue trees of #305 and each required feature; the code at `c5cee1b` (= current `master` HEAD, confirmed identical via `compare/master...c5cee1b`).

## 1. Decomposition

**Required set as declared (#305 machine block):** `requires_features: [367, 368, 331, 341, 318]`, `requires_capstones: []`, `requires_tasks_exception: []`, `planned_features: []`. All five are filed and open. CAP-12 itself has zero native sub-issues, consistent with all five being shared features. The problems are in what the filed features actually contain:

- **The conditioning-block palette is unowned.** CAP-12's redirected design is ~5-6 drawn fixed-step conditioning blocks (transimpedance, high-pass, gain, low-pass, comparator) evaluated as closed-form IIR sections. No filed issue owns that scope. FEAT-049 (#331), the feature CAP-12 assigns it to "(narrowed)", was **not narrowed**: its filed abstract is still the full device-model programme — model cards over a generic `Part`, bipolar/MOSFET/JFET tiers, vendor `.subckt` ingestion as data, Newton limiting protocol, a 200-circuit convergence corpus. Its only filed tasks are #465 (TASK-0103, one diode family, "blocked on FEAT-046b's Newton/Jacobian types (**not filed**)") and #464 (TASK-0104, convergence hardening, "**DEFERRED TO FEAT-046b**"); TASK-0105 (module palette registration) is unfiled. A search of open issues for "transimpedance" / "conditioning block" returns no owner other than #305 itself. CAP-12's own roster admits this ("scope on #331 needs narrowing to match — reported separately, not edited here"); that narrowing never happened. **Every filed child of the required set could close and the block chain of §1 step 1 still could not be drawn.**
- **Per-view geometry is unowned.** CAP-12 requires FEAT-014 (#318) precisely for "per-view geometry in the shared model" (step 1's "analog half on its own view," AC-4's view-derived visibility, the minimality argument "remove FEAT-014's residual and the analog view's geometry overwrites the schematic view's"). But #318's own rewrite **moved per-view geometry out of scope**: "Per-view geometry is deliberately **not** built here: it is deferred to whichever issue first needs a second view (today, #329's breadboard canvas)" (§1 Out of scope). #318 now delivers content-derived net/group identity and instance paths only. Its sub-issue #383 (TASK-0036, the `VIEW`-section mechanism) is flagged inside #318 as **unreconciled** — "a maintainer still needs to re-scope, redirect, or close #383." Under #318's own rule, the first issue to need a second view is CAP-12's analog view — i.e. the deferral lands the design obligation back on this capstone, unfiled.
- **The real-time scalar is unowned.** CAP-12's roster describes FEAT-047 (#367) as "physical time base + nominal real-time scalar," and outcome step 2 requires "BPM printed **in real time**." #367's rewrite narrowed it to ratifying and fixing a 1 ps tick tree-wide (task #882); its body contains no real-time / wall-clock pacing content at all (grep over the full body: zero matches for real-time, scalar, wall-clock, pacing). The spec-in-seconds half CAP-12 needs is covered; the real-time pacing half of step 2 is covered by nothing in the required set.
- **The admission-slice carve partner is closed.** CAP-12's OQ-1 and OQ-3 say the fourth-`Element`-permit / `Put` widening decision "must be settled on **#303**" and the recommended default prices the widening "on #303's required row." #303 (CAP-11) was **closed 2026-08-03 as a duplicate of #308**, and #308's body explicitly drops all three analog features ("Deliberately not required — FEAT-046 (#351), FEAT-048 (#368), FEAT-049 (#331). None of them is needed…"). So the carve has no live second party: the widening is owned only by #331's un-id'd "residual," whose own Proposed disposition recommends extracting it as a standalone issue under #78 — which is **not filed**. #331's OQ-1 and re-planning protocol still cite #303 as a live carve partner (its rewrite is dated 2026-08-08/09, after #303 closed).
- No double-ownership was found — the failure mode here is zero owners, not two. FEAT-027 (#341, task #387) and the spec-in-seconds half of #367 (task #882) are the only two required features whose filed scope actually matches what CAP-12 needs from them.

## 2. Acceptance-criteria composition

- **The filed children verify a different design than the capstone accepts.** #331's integration criteria are stamp goldens, Newton limiting-protocol assertions, and a convergence-corpus ratchet; #368's crossing mechanism is defined by "bisecting the **accepted step**" of "a **solved trajectory**" from #351's timestep controller. Every filed child AC could pass — diode stamps green, limiter goldens green, solver-step bridges green — and CAP-12's AC-1 through AC-4 would still be unbuildable, because none of those children produce a fixed-step block chain. Conversely, an implementation of CAP-12's actual design satisfies almost none of the filed children. This is a structural composition failure, not a wording gap.
- **AC-1** is well-derived (tolerance `|ΔT| ≤ T²/120` computed from the fixture's time base, never a constant), but its phrase "the fixture's **declared** time base (FEAT-047)" now contradicts #367's rewrite, whose title and body mandate "**no per-circuit declaration**" — a fixed 1 ps quantum. Reword to the fixture's declared *sample rate* over the fixed quantum; low severity but it is the AC's anchor sentence.
- **AC-4** derives palette visibility "from view identity" and names "FEAT-014's residual (the view identity visibility is derived from)" as one of its two spanning features. #318 no longer supplies view identity (above), and #331's filed mechanism derives visibility from **module activation** (`gui.palette-contributor` seam + `Activation` policy), not from views — #331 invariant 2 and §1 item 3. Same outcome (first-year sees nothing), different mechanism and different spanning feature; AC-4's span must be re-derived or it tests a mechanism nobody is building.
- **Step 2's "in real time"** appears in no AC and no child criterion (see §1); as written the walk-through can fail while AC-1..AC-6 all pass.
- **AC-5** (architecture-of-record gate) and **AC-6** (block-chain-vs-solver spike) are well-formed, have real pass/fail outcomes, and correctly mirror #331's OQ-6 and the CAP-14 (#309) escape hatch. These two are the right first work items — they are executable today without any of the missing decomposition.
- The DoD is strong (re-costing, carve settlement, evidence re-resolution, machine-block agreement at close) and would catch most of the above at *close* — but this review gates *start*, and the gaps above prevent start.

## 3. Dependency chains

- **Acyclic: yes.** Every `blocked_by`/`blocks` edge I walked is mirrored on its counterpart (#351 `blocks:[331,368]` ↔ #331 `blocked_by:[351]` ↔ #368 `blocked_by:[331,351]`; #331 `blocks:[368,464]`; #341 `blocked_by:[322]` ; #318 `blocked_by:[468]`). No cycle found; no edge points at a closed issue (the #303 references are prose/OQ references, not machine-block edges).
- **But the filed edges contradict the capstone's exclusion of FEAT-046.** CAP-12's machine block states "#351 is NOT required by this capstone's default design," and its mermaid removes the node. As filed, **both #331 and #368 are hard-blocked by #351** — so #351 sits on CAP-12's critical path twice via transitive closure. The mermaid's claim that "F049 → F048 is the one intra-set ordering edge" is literally true intra-set but materially misleading: three required features are blocked by out-of-set open work (#351, #322, #468) that the graph does not surface. Severing the #331→#351 and #368→#351 edges is exactly the un-executed narrowing/re-derivation work from §1.
- **Unfiled prerequisite on the critical path:** #331's only two filed tasks (#465, #464) are blocked by "FEAT-046b" (the nonlinear engine), which **does not exist as an issue** (title search: no hits). An unfiled blocker is worse than a closed one — it cannot even be scheduled.
- **The governance gate is real and currently failed.** #331 (OQ-6, disposition) and #368 (inherited-gap note) both state no child may be *authorized to land* — #331 says even *filed* — until `docs/capability-roadmap/README.md` §6(a), `docs/grand-architecture.md`, and an `ARCHITECTURE.md` recorded decision are amended. Neither document is amended on master. CAP-12's AC-5 correctly internalizes this; it is an internal prerequisite (docs + a maintainer decision), not an external one, so the verdict is not-ready rather than blocked.
- #341 additionally sits behind #322 (FEAT-026, open) and #318 behind #468 (TASK-0007, open) — real, funded, in-repo work; fine, but it belongs in CAP-12's risk picture.
