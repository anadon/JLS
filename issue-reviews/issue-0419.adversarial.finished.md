## Findings, most severe first

A net carries values and width; it does not carry an alphabet."* None of §7.4, §7.5, §7.10, §8's checklist bullets ("Write one private radix-agreement predicate ... call it from both `makeNet` and `recheck`"), or §14's Definition-of-Done bullet ("the single radix-agreement predicate ... hold as declared") were struck from the body. A contributor who executes §8 literally builds the exact design the maintainer just vetoed; a contributor who follows the comments instead silently fails several checked boxes in §14. Recommendation: do not hand this issue to an implementer until the body itself (not just comments) is edited to the ports-only model, with §7.4/§7.5/§7.10/§9's `RadixConnectionTest` assertion and §14's predicate bullet rewritten or removed.
