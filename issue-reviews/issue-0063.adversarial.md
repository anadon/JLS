Everything past that — the element, the subprocess harness, `docs/hdl-cosimulation.md` — is unimplemented: `git grep -ln HdlComponent origin/master -- src/ test/` returns nothing, `docs/hdl-cosimulation.md` does not exist, and `git grep -ln ProcessBuilder origin/master -- src/` returns nothing. ## Findings, most severe first

**1. A load-bearing design decision is unresolved and self-contradictory, and the body still presents it as settled.** The 2026-07-17 decision ledger (kept "binding" in the current body) commits to *delta-cycle to convergence* through JLS's own post/react settle loop, with P3 requiring interactive latency (well under 10 ms) for 3–5 components. The 2026-08-04/08-08 absorption comments (from the merged #360/#420 material) assert the opposite — a strictly **forward-only** contract ($t_{n+1}\ge t_n$, never re-entered) — and give a structural reason: `git grep -in "rollback|withdraw|uncommit" -- src/jls/sim/` returns nothing (verified independently), so a convergence loop that needs to re-poll a subprocess at a time already committed has no engine support and "would have to build [rollback] first, which is a different and much larger feature." Two separate comments (5175745158, 5176035718) flag this explicitly — *"These cannot both be the plan of record… Adjudicate here before the harness is funded"* — and as of the newest comment (2026-08-08 18:17) it is still open. **Recommendation:** do not let a future contributor build against the body's ledger as written; post a binding adjudication comment (delta-cycle vs. forward-only) and edit §3/§5/the Open Questions ledger before T3/T4 work starts, retiring whichever of P3 (interactive latency) or the forward-only assertion test loses.

**2. The machine block is stale relative to the issue's own comment thread, in violation of its own Definition of Done.** The body's YAML block still reads `blocked_by: []` and `serves_capstones: [59]`. But: (a) comment 5176035718 states plainly *"`blocked_by` becomes **[339, 359]**"* after absorbing #360, and #339 is confirmed open with a real, currently-unaddressed gap (`ScannedPort.Direction` has `{IN,OUT,INOUT}`, `HdlModel.Direction` has only `{INPUT,OUTPUT}` — verified on current master); (b) #59, the sole entry in `serves_capstones`, is **CLOSED** (confirmed via `issue_read`), and the issue's own newest comment (5227043130) says so and flags that the DoD line *"every capstone in serves_capstones notified"* "cannot be discharged as written." The DoD explicitly requires *"Machine block, roster table, and mermaid graph agree with reality at close"* — they don't agree with reality now, mid-issue, after 13 comments of bookkeeping. **Recommendation:** treat the body as untrustworthy for dependency/status queries until a maintainer edit reconciles the YAML block with the comment thread; anyone triaging this issue by reading only the body will get a wrong picture of what blocks it.

**3. The evidence commit underlying the most detailed scope statement (#420, absorbed into this issue's record) is not on the default branch and is slated for deletion.** `git merge-base --is-ancestor 2d0ca9dcd9db78b36c3caf4c6c57dd8701862cc7 origin/master` fails; that commit exists only on `remotes/origin/claude/jls-virtual-hardware-linux-njsoma`. The issue's own final comment (5227464420) already caught this: *"#420 declares `evidence_commit: 2d0ca9d`, which #493 records as branch-only and scheduled for deletion. Re-derive the scanner's existence and shape against master first."* Multiple absorbed comments (5175745158, 5226951211) cite line-numbered permalinks against that commit (e.g. `HdlModel.java#L28-L33`, `Element.java#L344-L351`), which is a direct violation of this issue's own DoD line *"Every cited evidence document and permalink resolves on the default branch at close."* Some of those permalinks do still resolve against current master by coincidence (spot-checked `HdlModel.java`, `ScannedPort.java`), but that is not guaranteed and was never re-verified after the branch-deletion warning. **Recommendation:** re-derive every O1–O8 observation against `origin/master` before relying on it further; do not treat unverified branch-only commit citations as settled fact.

**4. P3 (interactive latency) is effectively untestable in its current form and is silently at risk of never being adjudicated either way.** Sub-10ms round-trip latency for a component whose "reaction" is a full stdin/stdout exchange with an external `iverilog`/`ghdl` subprocess (cross-platform, including Windows/macOS per CI) is an aggressive bound with no stated baseline or measurement methodology, and it is exactly the criterion in question in Finding 1's unresolved contradiction. Because nobody has explicitly retired it, an implementer could ship the forward-only contract, never attempt convergence, and simply never reach the point where P3 is tested — leaving it permanently "not yet disproven" rather than pass or fail. **Recommendation:** P3 should not remain a Definition-of-Done gate until Finding 1 is settled; make its disposition (kept, retired, or redefined for forward-only semantics) an explicit output of that adjudication.

**5. T1 (Yosys `write_json` fallback) has no owner despite two dedup passes disagreeing about whether it's covered.** Comment 5226951211 (2026-08-08 16:15) claimed #420 "folds T1 in… as the routing rule"; the very next comment (5227043130, 16:39, 24 minutes later) retracts this by quoting #420's own §13 ("Out of scope: … growing the scanner") and §10 (the fallback is entered "only on refutation," i.e. contingent, not planned work). The retraction is correct, but it means T1 — explicitly declared "mutually independent and safe for concurrent agents" in §6 — is real, separately-schedulable scope with **no filed child issue and no owner**, after 13 comments of triage specifically about this feature. **Recommendation:** file the T1 task explicitly rather than continuing to resolve its ownership by inference across comment reversals.

**6. Process overhead has substantially outpaced delivered engineering.** 13 comments (all machine-attributed, "Generated by Claude Code") spanning merges, an absorption, and a same-day absorption-then-withdrawal of #420, yet zero lines of T2/T3/T4 implementation exist on master beyond the already-landed scanner. This is not disqualifying on its own, but it is worth naming as a signal: the specification-to-code ratio here is extreme, and each new bookkeeping pass has introduced its own errors (the DAG-walk overstatement corrected on #360, the #420-duplicate misjudgment reversed within the hour) that a human maintainer has not yet had to adjudicate directly. **Recommendation:** before funding T2/T3, have a human (not another automated pass) confirm the roster, the blocked_by edges, and Finding 1's contract choice in one sitting.

## What's solid

- The scanner work (T2's prerequisite) is real, tested, and cross-checked against Yosys ground truth — verified present and merged into `origin/master`.
- The subprocess-only integration boundary for GHDL (GPLv3) / Icarus (GPLv2) is the right call and is consistent with the project's recorded plugin-trust-boundary decision (ARCHITECTURE.md, #222) — it sidesteps GPL in-process-linking hazards cleanly.
- The five-state canvas UX (missing/stale/unavailable/crashed/normal) with a required one-line cause+remedy tooltip per state is concretely specified and each state is paired with a plannable, headless-assertable test — a good acceptance-criteria pattern, once Finding 1 is resolved underneath it.
- CI's skip-when-absent idiom for external tools (`ToolLocator`, best-effort apt install in `ci.yml`) is a sound, already-proven pattern this feature correctly plans to reuse.

## Bottom line

The idea and the already-landed half are sound. The issue as currently written cannot safely be picked up by a new contributor: its own machine-readable status block contradicts its comment thread, its binding design decision contradicts itself across two irreconcilable transport contracts, and part of its cited evidence sits on a branch marked for deletion. These are exactly the kinds of defects that make an issue look "fully speced" while actually being unsafe to implement against.
